PROJECT 1 GOLD — DESIGN DOCUMENT

NEW MECHANIC: SPEED TILES

Our new mechanic introduces two types of floor tiles: Fast Tiles and Slow Tiles. Fast Tiles increase Link's movement speed, while Slow Tiles decrease it. These tiles change how players navigate the dungeon, avoid enemies, and decide whether to collect resources.

TEACHING THE MECHANIC

Room 1 introduces the mechanic through a simple slow zone and fast zone. Players can experience both movement effects before using the fast zone to proceed to Room 2.

EXPLORING THE MECHANIC

Room 2 introduces enemies and a fast pathway surrounded by slow zones. Rupees and hearts are placed inside the slow zones, creating a risk-reward decision: players can stay on the faster route or enter dangerous slow areas to collect resources.

Room 3 builds on Room 2 with increased difficulty and Wallmasters acting as traps. Players must carefully manage their movement speed to avoid enemies and navigate the room.

Room 4 combines extensive fast and slow zones into a final challenge. Players must apply the movement strategies learned in the previous rooms.

DESIGN GOAL

The level gradually introduces the mechanic, increases difficulty, and encourages meaningful choices between speed, safety, and rewards.
